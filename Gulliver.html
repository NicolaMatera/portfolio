Gulliver - IKEA

I design digital experiences for people.

A custom promotional WordPress website designed to showcase a specific IKEA product. From showcasing product value to driving the purchase decision

GitHub

The Context

At a Glance

The Challenge

The goal was to design a web interface that not only highlighted the product's unique features but also encouraged conversions. This was achieved by harmoniously integrating the principles of interaction design, design, and user psychology.

Reference university course

Target

The target audience consisted of IKEA customers with an affinity for the product. Their needs were deeply analyzed through detailed User Personas to guide the design strategy

The Execution

Built on WordPress with customizations allowed only through HTML, CSS, PHP, and JS, the site was developed according to requirements at a resolution of 1920px. The requested deliverables included a User Persona, Moodboard, Wireframe, and Figma Mockup.

Results & Reflections

Looking Back

Applying interaction design and user psychology was rewarding, but the project has constraints: responsiveness is limited to three fixed breakpoints, and user research lacked real behavioral data.
